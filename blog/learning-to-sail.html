All team news September 2, 2026

New to sailing? You’re in good company.

About half of our team had never sailed before coming to Bates. If that’s you, here’s what your first couple of weeks on Taylor Pond will look like.

Day one: learning the boat

We start mellow. You’ll learn the parts of a Flying Junior and how to rig one, so you know what everything does before you ever leave the dock.

Week one: jumping in

From there, we encourage new sailors to get out on the water with experienced teammates as much as possible. They’ll show you the basics, like trimming the jib and moving through tacks and jibes. Then, honestly, we throw you into the deep end. It’s the fastest way to learn, and it works.

By the end of the fall

Plenty of first-years are skippering or crewing at regattas.

Getting there

Monday through Thursday, we meet outside FSU at 4:00 and carpool together to Taylor Pond, where we sail. No car needed, just show up and we’ll get you there.

What to bring

Layers you don’t mind getting wet, sneakers or water shoes, and a water bottle. We’ll lend you a life jacket and anything else you need.

Bates Sailing Team, Bates College, Lewiston, Maine. sailing@groups.bates.edu and @batessailing
