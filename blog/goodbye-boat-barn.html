Saying goodbye to the Boat Barn

It's with a heavy heart that we share that Bates Facilities Services and Maintenance have set a demolition date this summer for our Boat Barn on Frye Street.

We'll miss our little building more than anything, and we're grateful for all the memories it has held over the past 44 years.

It's hard news, but we're looking forward to working with the college on a new space, and to the bigger, brighter future Bates Club Sailing has ahead.

Share your Boat Barn stories

Alumni, we'd love to hear your favorite Boat Barn memories for an upcoming issue of the Bi-Monthly. Email them to sailing@groups.bates.edu.
