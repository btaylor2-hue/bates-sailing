Sail with us on Taylor Pond.

We're a coed, student-run club team from Bates College. We practice on Taylor Pond and race college regattas across New England every fall. Most practices end as the sun drops behind the trees.

Fun, competitive, and open to every Bobcat

More than 50 of Bates's 1,800 students sail with us. About half of them had never sailed before they joined.

  • We're a fun team

    When the wind is too light on Taylor Pond, you'll find us at Wallingford's eating apple cider donuts. After practice, we eat dinner together in Commons.

  • We're a competitive team

    We race 15+ regattas a year as members of NEISA, the most active conference in college sailing, with around eight regattas every fall weekend.

  • We're a club team

    As a Bates club sport, any student can join. Whether you want to race or just want an afternoon on the water after class, there's a spot for you.

Where we sail

Our home is Taylor Pond Yacht Club in Auburn, about 15 minutes from campus across the Androscoggin River. The team owns 15 Flying Juniors, the two-person boat most New England colleges race.

Frequently asked questions

Still wondering about something? Email us and a sailor will get back to you.

sailing@groups.bates.edu
Who can join the team?

Any Bates student. We're a club sport within Bates Athletics, so there are no tryouts, and about half our sailors start with no experience.

Where do we sail?

At Taylor Pond Yacht Club in Auburn, about 15 minutes from campus across the Androscoggin River. We meet outside FSU at 4:00 and carpool there together, so you don't need a car.

What boats do we sail?

The team owns 15 collegiate Flying Juniors (FJs). They're 13 feet long, sail with two people, and are the most common college racing boat in New England.

When do we practice?

Monday through Thursday from 4:00 to 6:30 PM, or until sundown if that's earlier.

The fall season runs from the start of classes until early November. Spring practice runs through April and May, starting once the ice is out.

Do I need to buy sailing gear?

No. We provide a Coast Guard approved life jacket, and the team has used gear and drysuits to lend. You may already own useful layers like fleece and synthetics.

Most long-term sailors eventually buy their own dinghy boots and spray pants. If you have your own life jacket or gear, bring it.

Who coaches the team?

Peter Garcia, our long-time coach and Auburn's 2010 Man of the Year. He has more time on the water than most sailors manage in a lifetime.

Do we race other schools?

Yes. We're part of NEISA, the New England conference of the Intercollegiate Sailing Association, which runs about eight regattas every fall weekend. Most events are coed and some are women's regattas. Results are on Techscore.

Why should I join?

For the people. This team is special because of the people and the community we build every year. It’s something you don’t want to miss.

Bates Sailing is a coed, student-run club team at Bates College in Lewiston, Maine. We sail at Taylor Pond Yacht Club in Auburn.
